# Anyra's TV: parent guide

Anyra's TV shows only the channels you choose. There is no search, no home feed and no YouTube suggestions. Every phone with the app shows the same channel list.

## Put it on your iPhone (once)

1. Open **Safari** and go to the app's address (Urjita will send it).
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow), scroll down, and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Anyra's TV from the new icon on your home screen.

## Lock the phone to Anyra's TV (Guided Access)

Turn this on once, then use it every time she watches. It stops her from leaving the app, including by tapping the YouTube logo.

1. Go to **Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access** and turn it **on**.
2. Tap **Passcode Settings > Set Guided Access Passcode** and pick a code only grown-ups know.

**Each time she watches:** open Anyra's TV, then **press the side button three times quickly** and tap **Start**.
**To finish:** press the side button three times, enter the code, and tap **End**.

## Add or remove channels

1. On the Anyra's TV home screen, tap the **gear** in the top corner.
2. Enter the **parent PIN**.
3. **To add:** type the channel's name (like "Ms Rachel"), tap **Search**, check the picture matches, and tap **Add**.
   If search can't find it, open the channel in the YouTube app, tap **Share > Copy link**, and paste it in the second box.
4. **To remove:** tap **Remove**, then tap it again to confirm.
5. Tap **Done** when finished. The other phones get the change next time they open the app.

You can have up to 20 channels.

## What to expect

- **Ads can appear** before or during some videos. YouTube controls this, and kids' videos only get basic, non-personalized ads.
- **Shorts are hidden** on purpose.
- When a video ends, a big replay button appears and her channel's other videos are just below it.

## If something goes wrong

- **"No internet"**: check Wi-Fi, then tap **Try again**.
- **Forgot the PIN**: ask Urjita. The channel list is also in the shared Google Sheet "Anyra's TV channels."
- **A video is not OK for her**: remove that channel for now. (Hiding single videos may come later.)
